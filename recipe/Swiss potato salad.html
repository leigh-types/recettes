Swiss potato salad

.cook
👥 4 servings

🥘 Ingredients

Vinaigrette

  • Aromat
    to taste
  • Maggi liquid seasoning
    1 tsp
  • Swiss Kressi Vinegar
    3 tbsp
  • Swiss Thomy mustard
    1 tsp
  • olive oil
    3 tbsp
  • onion (finely chopped)
    1/2
  • pepper
    to taste
  • salt
    to taste

Salad

  • beef bouillon (warm)
    1/4 cup
  • potatoes (skin on)
    4

Vinaigrette

  1. 1
    In a medium bowl, combine Swiss Kressi Vinegar , olive oil , Swiss Thomy mustard , Maggi liquid seasoning , onion , salt , pepper and Aromat .
    Swiss Kressi Vinegar: 3 tbsp, olive oil: 3 tbsp, Swiss Thomy mustard: 1 tsp, Maggi liquid seasoning: 1 tsp, onion: 1/2 (finely chopped), salt: to taste, pepper: to taste, Aromat: to taste

Salad

  1. 📝

    For best results, boil the evening before and store in fridge

  2. 1
    Boil potatoes in a large pot for 20 to 30 minutes.
    potatoes: 4 (skin on)
  3. 2
    Peel potatoes the next morning. Slice potatoes and put in a big bowl. Add beef bouillon . Mix and add the vinaigrette.
    beef bouillon: 1/4 cup (warm)
