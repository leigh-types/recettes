One-pot ginger chicken and rice

.cook
#asian chicken and rice #chicken and mushroom rice #Chicken and Rice #hong kong chicken and rice

Recipe video above. This one-pot ginger chicken and rice is inspired by the flavours of Hong Kong claypot rice, steamed with a layer of mushrooms and finished with a drizzle of sauce. It's gone straight into my top 3 one-pot recipes!I've used a mix of Asian mushrooms here which have more interesting textures and impart more flavour into the rice than regular white mushrooms. But I wouldn't hesitate to make this with any type of mushrooms!

👥 4 servings 🍽️ Mains ⏱️ Prep Time: 10 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 25 minutes 🌍 Asian, Chinese 📖 www.recipetineats.com

🥘 Ingredients

Sauce

  • chicken thighs (boneless, skinless, cut into small bite size pieces)
    500 g
  • fish sauce
    2 tbsp
  • kecap manis
    3 tbsp
  • white pepper
    1/8 tsp

Mushrooms

  • oil
    2.5 tbsp
  • salt
  • shiitake mushrooms (fresh, small halved, large thickly sliced)
    150 g
  • white pepper

Chicken

  • garlic (finely minced)
    2 cloves
  • ginger (grated)
    1.5 tbsp
  • green onion stems (finely sliced)
  • long grain rice (NOT RINSED)
    1.5 cups
  • oil
    1 tbsp

Simmer

  • chicken stock (low sodium)
    2 cups
  • ginger (~5mm / 0.2" thick, no need to peel)
    4-5 slices

Drizzle and Toss

  • green onion stems (cut into 5cm/2" lengths)
    4

🍳 Cookware

  • large heavy based pot

Sauce

  1. 1
    Mix kecap manis , fish sauce , and white pepper in a small bowl. Toss chicken thighs with 1 1/2 tbsp of the sauce, set the rest aside for drizzling.
    kecap manis: 3 tbsp, fish sauce: 2 tbsp, white pepper: 1/8 tsp, chicken thighs: 500 g (boneless, skinless, cut into small bite size pieces)

Mushrooms

  1. 1
    Heat oil in a large heavy based pot on high heat. Cook half the shiitake mushrooms with a pinch of salt and white pepper until some of the mushrooms have a light tinge of golden on the surface, but don't try to cook them all the way through. Remove into a bowl. Repeat with remaining mushrooms.
    oil: 2.5 tbsp, shiitake mushrooms: 150 g (fresh, small halved, large thickly sliced), salt, white pepper

Chicken

  1. 1
    Heat the remaining oil until hot. Add the chicken and stir for 1 minute, just until the surface is sealed but inside still raw. Add the white part of green onion stems , garlic , and ginger , stir for 30 seconds. Add long grain rice , stir briefly just to coat in oil.
    oil: 1 tbsp, green onion stems (finely sliced), garlic: 2 cloves (finely minced), ginger: 1.5 tbsp (grated), long grain rice: 1.5 cups (NOT RINSED)

Simmer

  1. 1
    Add chicken stock and ginger . Bring to a rapid simmer. Scatter mushrooms across the surface (add any pooled liquid too), then let it come to a rapid simmer again.
    chicken stock: 2 cups (low sodium), ginger: 4-5 slices (~5mm / 0.2" thick, no need to peel)

Steam

  1. 1
    Put the lid on, reduce stove to low (or medium low, for weak burners). Cook for ⏱️ 20 minutes (do not stir) until the liquid is fully absorbed.

Rest

  1. 1
    Remove pot from the stove with the lid still on and rest for ⏱️ 10 minutes .

Drizzle and Toss

  1. 1
    Drizzle remaining sauce all over, add the green part of green onion stems . Gently toss until the green onion is mostly wilted. Serve!
    green onion stems: 4 (cut into 5cm/2" lengths)
