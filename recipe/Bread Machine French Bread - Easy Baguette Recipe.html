Bread Machine French Bread

.cook

This bread machine french bread recipe is very easy to make. It only uses a few simple ingredients such as flour, water, salt and yeast. Moreover, this french bread tastes great and you can mold it into a beautiful baguette shape. Your family will be amazed by baking skills... while you smirk because you made it with the help of a bread machine! Visit BreadDad.com for more great bread machine recipes.

🍽️ Appetizer, Dinner, Lunch, Sandwich ⏱️ Prep Time: 2 hours 40 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 20 minutes 🌍 American, European 👤 Bread Dad 📖 breaddad.com

🥘 Ingredients

  • bread flour
    420 g
  • bread machine yeast
    7.2 g
  • olive oil
    2 tbsp
  • salt
    6 g
  • water
    307 ml

🍳 Cookware

  • bread machine
  • nonstick baking sheet
  1. 1
    Unplug the bread machine and then remove the bread pan.
  2. 2
    Place water and then the rest of the ingredients into the bread pan. You can make a little indent on top of the bread flour in order to avoid the bread machine yeast falling into the water (until the machine starts). Do not combine the salt with the bread machine yeast (as the salt can kill the bread machine yeast ). Place the bread machine yeast and salt in separate parts of the bread pan.
    water: 307 ml, bread flour: 420 g, bread machine yeast: 7.2 g, salt: 6 g, bread machine yeast, salt, bread machine yeast, bread machine yeast, salt
  3. 3
    Place the bread pan back into the bread machine and then plug in the bread machine.
  4. 4
    Put your machine on the "Dough" setting and press the start button. In our bread machine, the kneading and rising on the dough setting takes about ⏱️ 90 minutes .
  5. 5
    When your bread machine has finished, unplug the machine and pour the dough onto a cutting board. Wear oven mitts (as the bread pan/bread machine may be hot). Sprinkle some bread flour on the cutting board (before you pour the dough) in order to avoid the dough sticking to the cutting board.
    bread flour
  6. 6
    Divide the dough into 4 equal parts if you want to make 4 skinny baguettes (roughly 12 inches long). Or divide the dough into 2 equal parts in order to make 2 thicker french bread "rolls". FYI - I usually make 2 thicker and longer loaves with this recipe.
  7. 7
    Roll the divided dough with your hands into your preferred shape (i.e. baguette or thicker bread roll). See our tips below on shaping the bread dough (especially about making the dough roll about 1/2 the width of your desired end product... as the dough expands as the bread machine yeast rises).
    bread machine yeast
  8. 8
    Place the shaped dough onto a nonstick baking sheet .
  9. 9
    Coat the dough with olive oil . Use a small pastry brush.
    olive oil: 2 tbsp
  10. 10
    Cover the dough and baking sheet with a light kitchen cloth or plastic wrap in order to protect from dust, etc.
  11. 11
    Let the dough rise for ⏱️ 45-60 minutes . FYI - This time can vary. The speed of the rise is based in part on the temperature of your kitchen (e.g. rise faster in the summer and slower in the winter). See the "poke test" info in the tips section below on how to determine if your dough has risen/proofed to the correct level (and it is not under or over proofed). I like to start my poke testing around ⏱️ 35-40 minutes (as I would rather be too early versus too late).
  12. 12
    During this "rising" time, preheat your oven to 450 degrees F.
  13. 13
    After the ⏱️ 45-60 minutes , remove the covering from the dough and "score" (slice) the top of each dough roll with a sharp knife. Make diagonal slices about 1/2 inch deep and about 3 inches apart. This will help prevent the bread from cracking during baking. See the tips below about how to score bread.
  14. 14
    Place the baking sheet in the oven. It should bake at 450 degrees for ⏱️ 15-20 minutes or until golden brown. Wear oven mitts.
  15. 15
    At the ⏱️ 8-10 minutes mark, turn the baking sheet around in order to ensure an even "browning" of the bread. Wear oven mitts.
  16. 16
    Remove the baking sheet when done and place the bread on a cooling rack. Wear oven mitts.
